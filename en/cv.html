Curriculum vitae

I graduated from the Model Evangelical School of Nea Smyrni and entered the School of Dentistry of the University of Athens in 1983. After working in my own private practice as a general dentist from 1991 to 1993, in 1993 I was accepted into the official clinical residency program in Endodontics of the University of Medicine & Dentistry of New Jersey (now Rutgers University, Newark, N.J., USA), from which I graduated in the summer of 1996.

From 1996 to this day I have practised full-time, exclusively and only the specialty of Endodontics in my practice. Beyond my clinical work, I have from time to time taught seminars for dentists across Greece and Cyprus.
