Root canal retreatment

Root canal retreatment is a procedure we carry out when the previous root canal treatment was not done correctly and the root canals were not properly cleaned, shaped, disinfected and filled.

It is clearly a more complex procedure than the initial root canal treatment, as the specialist has to correct everything that went wrong in the first one.

Like a standard root canal treatment, retreatment is, as a rule, completed in a single appointment. The following days are the same as after a standard treatment: the tooth is slightly tender to pressure, and we manage this easily with common anti-inflammatories. Antibiotics are not often needed.

Clearly more complexthan the initial treatment — and yet, as a rule, a single appointment.
